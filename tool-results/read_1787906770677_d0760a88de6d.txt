     1→# MASTER PROMPT — BUILD A FULL-STACK UGC NET PREPARATION PLATFORM
     2→
     3→## 1. PROJECT VISION
     4→
     5→Build a **production-ready, exceptionally helpful, modern full-stack web platform for UGC NET aspirants**.
     6→
     7→The platform should become a student's **complete digital preparation ecosystem for UGC NET**, where they can:
     8→
     9→* Discover UGC NET information and strategy
    10→* Select their Paper II subject
    11→* Access books and study material
    12→* Read detailed notes
    13→* Download concise cheat sheets
    14→* Study topic-by-topic
    15→* Practice MCQs
    16→* Attempt quizzes and mock tests
    17→* Solve previous-year questions
    18→* Bookmark important resources
    19→* Track preparation progress
    20→* Create a personalized study plan
    21→* Monitor weak and strong topics
    22→* Maintain study streaks
    23→* Take notes
    24→* Search the entire knowledge base
    25→* Prepare for Paper I and Paper II from one place
    26→
    27→The website must feel like a combination of:
    28→
    29→**UGC NET Library + Digital Notes Platform + MCQ Practice App + Mock Test Platform + Study Planner + Progress Dashboard + Knowledge Base**
    30→
    31→Do not build a generic educational website.
    32→
    33→Build a product that an aspirant could realistically use every day during their UGC NET preparation.
    34→
