    17→    17→* Solve previous-year questions
    18→    18→* Bookmark important resources
    19→    19→* Track preparation progress
    20→    20→* Create a personalized study plan
    21→    21→* Monitor weak and strong topics
    22→    22→* Maintain study streaks
    23→    23→* Take notes
    24→    24→* Search the entire knowledge base
    25→    25→* Prepare for Paper I and Paper II from one place
    26→    26→
    27→    27→The website must feel like a combination of:
    28→    28→
    29→    29→**UGC NET Library + Digital Notes Platform + MCQ Practice App + Mock Test Platform + Study Planner + Progress Dashboard + Knowledge Base**
    30→    30→
