# MASTER PROMPT — BUILD A FULL-STACK UGC NET PREPARATION PLATFORM

## 1. PROJECT VISION

Build a **production-ready, exceptionally helpful, modern full-stack web platform for UGC NET aspirants**.

The platform should become a student's **complete digital preparation ecosystem for UGC NET**, where they can:

* Discover UGC NET information and strategy
* Select their Paper II subject
* Access books and study material
* Read detailed notes
* Download concise cheat sheets
* Study topic-by-topic
* Practice MCQs
* Attempt quizzes and mock tests
* Solve previous-year questions
* Bookmark important resources
* Track preparation progress
* Create a personalized study plan
* Monitor weak and strong topics
* Maintain study streaks
* Take notes
* Search the entire knowledge base
* Prepare for Paper I and Paper II from one place

The website must feel like a combination of:

**UGC NET Library + Digital Notes Platform + MCQ Practice App + Mock Test Platform + Study Planner + Progress Dashboard + Knowledge Base**

Do not build a generic educational website.

Build a product that an aspirant could realistically use every day during their UGC NET preparation.

---

# 2. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The primary goal is:

> "Help a UGC NET aspirant go from syllabus → learning → revision → practice → analysis → improvement → exam readiness."

Every feature, page, database model, and UI decision should support this journey.

The website should answer common student questions immediately:

* What should I study?
* Which topic should I study today?
* Where are my notes?
* Which book should I use?
* What is important for the exam?
* How much of the syllabus have I completed?
* Which topics am I weak in?
* What questions were asked previously?
* How am I performing?
* What should I revise?
* What should I do next?
